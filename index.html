Your first 90 days

You will know what to do at every session. Here is what to expect.

  1. Day 1

    Assessment and baseline

    We test your movement, record your starting lifts, and set one clear goal with a date on it.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4

    Build the foundation

    Technique first. Most members hit their first strength personal records by week three.

  3. Weeks 5 to 8

    Add load

    Weights go up, sessions get sharper, and you start to see changes in the mirror at home.

  4. Weeks 9 to 12

    Test and reset

    Re-test everything from day one, compare the numbers, and plan the next block with your coach.
